## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the intersection of rigorous scientific pharmacology and artisanal specialty coffee culture. The personality is authoritative, refined, mindful, and visually enriching—treating biochemical caffeine data with the typographic reverence of a high-end specialty journal combined with the tactile warmth of a master roastery.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Editorial Typography** with **Warm Minimalist Tactility**. It rejects clinical sterility in favor of organic precision: rich botanical and roasted undertones, immaculate metric cards, high-contrast infographics, and serene editorial breathing room. The visual hierarchy communicates exactitude through meticulous data tables, biological curve diagrams, and stepped metric dials that feel scholarly yet deeply accessible.

## Colors

The color palette grounds scientific data in the natural spectrum of the coffee plant, roasting stages, and parchment lab notes:

- **Primary (`#2B1810` - Rich Espresso Brown)**: Anchors headline typography, primary buttons, structural lines, and authoritative chart lines.
- **Secondary (`#C88242` - Crema Amber)**: Highlights peak bio-activity, warnings within acceptable thresholds (e.g., 200–300 mg), interactive toggles, and hero accents.
- **Tertiary (`#4A6B53` - Botanical Moss)**: Represents safe baseline metrics, organic coffee botanical data (Arabica/Liberica attributes), and metabolic health verifications.
- **Neutral (`#F9F6F0` - Warm Ivory Parchment)**: Forms the core canvas, reducing eye fatigue from prolonged reading while conveying tactile warmth over harsh clinical white.

### Data Metric Semantics

- **Safe / Optimal (`botanical-moss` #4A6B53)**: 0–200 mg zone, baseline curves.
- **Elevated Performance (`crema-amber` #C88242)**: 200–300 mg zone, active metabolic half-life zones.
- **Caution / Threshold (`warning-amber` #D96B27)**: 300–400 mg daily limit indicators.
- **Toxic / Overdose (`danger-roast` #9E2A2B)**: >400 mg and pharmacological risk highlights.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes distinct voices for narrative guidance, scientific explanation, and telemetry:

- **Editorial Voice (Newsreader)**: Evokes classic botanical journals, medical treatises, and literary monographs. Used for primary section titles, hero introductions, and philosophical quote leads. It features expressive optical sizing with soft organic serifs.
- **Analytical Body & Numerical Telemetry (Manrope)**: Delivers optimal reading cadence for scientific explanations, metabolic biological mechanisms, dosage numbers, milligram ratios, chemical formulas (CYP1A2), and tabular comparison indices using OpenType tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

The layout adopts a fixed 12-column grid system capped at a maximum width of `1280px` for optimal data density without compromising editorial elegance.

### Structural Flow

- **Columns**: 12 columns on desktop (`>= 1024px`), 8 columns on tablet (`768px - 1023px`), 4 columns on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Data Densities**: Tabular conversion matrices (Arabica vs. Robusta, Half-life charts) occupy 12 full columns on mobile and 8-to-10 columns on desktop, flanked by analytical sidebars.
- **Interactive Calculators**: Arranged in an asymmetric 7:5 split on desktop, placing inputs and sliders on the left with instant dynamic chart visualizers on the right.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built upon a `0.5rem` (8px) base grid. Section dividers use hairline rules tinted with `slate-border` with a minimum `space-2xl` margin above and below.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic digital dropshadows, favoring **architectural layering**, **tactile low-contrast outlines**, and **subtle surface stacking**:

- **Ground Plane (`Level 0`)**: Tinted `#F9F6F0` (warm ivory canvas) sets an organic backdrop.
- **Surface Elevation (`Level 1`)**: Interactive cards, tables, and dosage calculators lift gently onto `#FFFFFF` tiles bounded by a crisp 1px border (`#E3DCD2`).
- **Interactive Hover (`Level 2`)**: Hovered cards or focused dosage selectors shift borders to `#C88242` and gain a diffused warm shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.07)`.
- **Floating Overlays (`Level 3`)**: Tooltips, dosage calculation flyouts, and modal glossary popups feature a subtle paper shadow: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.12)`, edged with `1px solid rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape grammar adheres to **Soft Precision (Level 1)**. Elements carry a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius, referencing precision lab glass, specimen microscope slides, and bookbinding edges rather than playful bubbly geometry.

- **Data Cards & Containers**: `4px` corner radius to retain clean structural grid lines.
- **Pills & Status Badges**: `9999px` full roundedness for organic biological tokens (e.g., Safe Dose, Toxic Zone, Arabica 1.2%).
- **Interactive Sliders & Scrubbers**: Flat rectangular pill tracks with circular thumb rings.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls

- **Primary Action (Dark Roast)**: Background `#2B1810`, text `#F9F6F0`, font `Manrope` medium 14px, radius 4px. Hover transforms to `#1B0F0A` with subtle lift.
- **Secondary Action (Crema Accent)**: Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #C88242`, text `#C88242`. Hover triggers tint fill `rgba(200, 130, 66, 0.08)`.
- **Segmented Range Toggles**: Tab groups framed in `#E3DCD2`. Active state uses solid `#FFFFFF` with bold typography and a bottom hairline accent in `#C88242`.

### Scientific Infographic Cards & Tables
- **Dosage Category Matrix**: Alternating rows using `#FFFFFF` and `rgba(249, 246, 240, 0.6)`. Right-aligned numeric values in `Manrope` with tabular figures. Status indicator dot using semantic colors (`botanical-moss`, `crema-amber`, `warning-amber`, `danger-roast`).
- **Half-Life Decay Visualizer**: Clean linear SVG graph plotting clearance over 24 hours. The baseline grid displays subtle dashed lines (`#E3DCD2`), and the plotted decay line uses a smooth 2.5px stroke of `#C88242` with shaded area fill in `rgba(200, 130, 66, 0.08)`.
- **Bean Comparison Cards**: Arabica vs. Robusta specimen tiles featuring botanical subtext, percentage breakdown pills, and side-by-side milligram gauges.
### Interactive Caffeine Calculator Card

- **Brew Variable Inputs**: Clean numeric stepper and slider modules with clear physical coffee cues (Gramatur Bubuk, Rasio Seduh, Ekstraksi).
- **Dynamic Metric Display**: Prominent real-time dosage total rendered in `metric-large` with an accompanying advisory status chip (e.g., "Aman untuk Dewasa", "Mendekati Batas Harian").

### Micro-Components & Badges

- **Biological Badges**: `table-header` styling, uppercase letter-spacing, padding `2px 8px`, border `1px solid` matching parent semantic tint.
- **Footnotes & Medical Disclaimers**: Displayed in an archival callout box bordered left with `3px solid #4A6B53`, rendered in `body-sm` with dimmed `#6E6862` typography.
